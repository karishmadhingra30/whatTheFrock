Browser-extension UI walkthrough

whatTheFrock

A product-page overlay prototype that detects garment composition and makes its scoring logic, source link, and material alternatives visible without disrupting a shopper’s flow.

Prototype boundary. The walkthrough below is a hardcoded simulation of a current Hanes basics product. The heuristic is not a validated exposure assessment or medical advice, and no retailer page or visitor data is read.
Try loading, low-, medium-, high-risk, and error states above. The full extension is available in the repository ↗.
